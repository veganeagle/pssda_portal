Pay change is the median change in salary paid for disclosed employees in the same job in both years (fewer than {{ min_compared }} in both years isn't shown); "vs inflation" is that change after Ontario CPI over the same years. It follows the same people, so new hires and retirements don't move it. Because they were already over $100,000 in the start year, most were at the top of their pay grid: their change mostly reflects negotiated scale increases, not step increases. University faculty also get annual merit and progress-through-the-ranks increases, which is part of why they lead. Retroactive pay can land in the end year or just before it, and salary paid includes overtime.
