Terms & disclaimer

OPSCI is a project of kdsay labs inc. It provides tools for exploring Ontario public-sector salary disclosures and related analysis.

Understanding the information

The original disclosures are reported by employers. OPSCI adds employee matching, job groupings and other estimates to help people explore patterns over time.

Errors may occur in the original records or in our analysis. The data is not a complete workforce list, and annual pay can be affected by overtime, leave, partial-year work and other circumstances. Please read About the data when interpreting the results.

Using the results

Use OPSCI as a starting point for research and comparison. Check the source records and relevant context before relying on a result for an important decision or publishing a claim about an individual.

Rankings show disclosed pay, not job performance. Linked histories and other estimates may contain errors.

Independence

OPSCI is independent of the Ontario government and the employers shown. Inclusion on the site does not imply their endorsement.

Corrections and updates

We may update records, matching rules, job groups and calculations as the data or methods change. If you believe something is wrong, please contact us.

Source information

The underlying data comes from Ontario's public-sector salary disclosures. If you reuse source data, check the terms that apply to that source. This page does not grant additional rights to government data.
