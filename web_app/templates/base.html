* "Disclosed" means required to appear on Ontario's Public Sector Salary Disclosure ("Sunshine List") — employees who earned $100,000 or more in salary that year. Disclosed payroll is the sum of salary paid to that group only (it excludes taxable benefits — it is not total compensation), and disclosed employees is a count of that group only. Neither figure reflects an employer's full workforce or full payroll spend, only the disclosed portion of it.
