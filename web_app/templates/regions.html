Regional Differences

Some public-sector jobs pay the same anywhere in Ontario; others pay noticeably more in one region. Each figure compares a job's median salary in a region with the same job's Ontario-wide median, so a high-paid job doesn't outweigh a lower-paid one. Region is where the employer is based.
